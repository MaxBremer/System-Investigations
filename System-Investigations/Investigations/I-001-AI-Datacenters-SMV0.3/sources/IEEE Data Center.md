## Key Takeaways

- The core subsystems of a data center, mapped here [[Data Center infrastructure.canvas]]
- Data center categories;
	- Enterprise: wholly owned and operated by one company.
	- Colocation: rent out the space, power, network, and security, but bring your own hardware. see [[Colocation]]
	- Managed Hosting: provide the location and the hardware, and lease the compute capacity on a contractual basis.
	- Cloud computing datacenters: Offer compute and/or storage resources on demand over the network, not physically.
