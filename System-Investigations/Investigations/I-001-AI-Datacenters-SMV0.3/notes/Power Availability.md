## Observation

Power availability and supply is a critical limiter to data center construction and operations. Greater power density requirements from AI workloads on servers lead the power requirements to scale faster, and transmission and infrastructure can't keep up.

Takes 18 to 24 months for a new datacenter to spring up. Takes 3 to 10 years for new power grid infrastructure to be established.

---

## Evidence

McKinsey article goes into it in some depth. Additionally, many hyperscalers are considering "behind-the-meter" options, essentially building their own power generation alongside the facility.
